AI Fellowship

Funding and technical mentorship for Harvard students pursuing their own AI research or software projects.

Application detailsSee fellows’ work

How it works

Funding can cover compute, API credits, and other project costs. Fellows meet with our technical directors every two weeks to work toward research or software they can share publicly.

Applying

Tell us the question you want to explore, how you’ll approach it, and what resources you’d need. The next application window will be announced when dates are available.
