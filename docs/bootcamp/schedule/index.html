Bootcamp schedule

Five days online

Here’s how the June 2026 cohorts spent the week. Dates and session times for the next cohort will be announced separately.

Back to Bootcamp

Day 1

Neural networks

Start with the basics of machine learning, then build a small neural network.

Day 2

Language models

Learn how transformers process text and explore what autoregressive language models can do.

Day 3

Computer vision

Work with convolutional networks and try a handwritten-digit classification exercise.

Day 4

Reinforcement learning and ethics

Explore how agents learn from rewards and discuss the decisions that shape their behavior.

Day 5

Project workshop

Develop a prototype in a small team and present a short demo.
