AI Bootcamp

A five-day online program for high school students, taught by Harvard undergraduate and graduate-level instructors.

Bootcamp detailsSee the schedule

Five-day outline

  1. Day 1Neural networks
  2. Day 2Language models
  3. Day 3Computer vision
  4. Day 4Reinforcement learning and ethics
  5. Day 5Project workshop

What you’ll do

Follow guided exercises and work on a small research project. The week ends with a workshop where you can turn an idea into a prototype and share what you built.

Common questions

Who is it for?

The bootcamp is designed for high school students interested in artificial intelligence and machine learning. Email the group for upcoming application dates and eligibility details.

Where does it take place?

Sessions are held online. The schedule shows what the last published summer cohort covered.

Who can I ask about the program?

Email ai@hcs.harvard.edu with questions about the schedule, curriculum, or application process.
